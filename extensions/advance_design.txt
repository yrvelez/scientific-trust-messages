Replicating the short student videos with a delayed follow-up and a behavioural outcome. 4 conditions: Control, Science through the day, Science in everyday life, Peer review video. Primary outcome: Immediate post-treatment trust in science (mean of 6 items, items 4-6 reverse-coded), with 7-day trust and the behavioural choice as secondary outcomes.. Population: US adults from an online panel such as Lucid, quota-matched to the adult population.
