Do the small positive videos replicate across samples and baseline trust?. 5 conditions: Control, Video 12, Video 15, Video 20, Comparison video. Primary outcome: Post-treatment trust in science, mean of the 6 items with items 4-6 reverse-coded, adjusted for pre-treatment trust..
